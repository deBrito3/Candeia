# The sweet I took

You must wonder why he leaves sweets, and why I wrote so much to keep you from taking them.

One night of that first year, the battery died before daybreak. I knew he was at the door. There was a sweet on the sill, and I was so tired that I opened the window and took the wrapper.

He went away at once. The house was silent until dawn.

The next morning, I could not remember your name. It came back in the afternoon, in pieces. Every sweet of his takes something in return, and I only learned what after I had lost it.

CANDEIA's hints are not his sweets. But I made the system charge for them all the same, so you would never forget that every shortcut has a price.
